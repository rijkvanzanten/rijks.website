# Rijk van Zanten

Rijk is a 🇳🇱 Dutch digital product designer and software developer based in 🗽 Brooklyn. He is the co-founder and CTO of 🐰 **Directus** ([directus.com](https://directus.com/)). In addition to his work at Directus, Rijk serves as a part-time 🤓 professor at 🎓 **Parsons School of Design** ([mpscd.parsons.edu](https://mpscd.parsons.edu/)) in New York City. When not at his desk, you can find him playing 🎸 bass in the Lower East Side or in the studio.
